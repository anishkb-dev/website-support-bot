# Brewing Guide

## Pour-over

Use 15 g of coffee to 250 g of water at 93-96 °C. Bloom with 40 g of water for 30 seconds, then pour slowly. Total brew time should be about 3 minutes.

## French press

Use 30 g of coarse-ground coffee to 500 g of water. Steep for 4 minutes, then press gently and serve right away.

## Storing your coffee

Keep beans in the resealable bag, away from light and heat. Don't freeze an opened bag. Coffee tastes best within 4 weeks of the roast date printed on the bag.
