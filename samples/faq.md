# Northwind Coffee Co. Help Center

Northwind Coffee Co. is a fictional small-batch coffee roaster used for this demo.

## Shipping

We roast every Monday and Thursday and ship within 1 business day of roasting.
Standard shipping (3-5 business days) is free on orders over $35; otherwise it costs $5.95.
Express shipping (1-2 business days) costs $12.
We currently ship to the US and Canada only.

## Returns

Unopened bags can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
If you don't love an opened bag, email us within 14 days and we'll send a different roast or refund you, no need to send it back.
Grinders and brewing equipment can be returned within 30 days if unused and in the original box.

## Subscriptions

Subscriptions deliver every 2, 3 or 4 weeks and save 10% on every bag.
You can pause, skip, or cancel anytime from My Account > Subscriptions, up to 2 days before the next roast date.

## Grind options

Every coffee is available as whole bean, or ground for espresso, pour-over, drip, French press or cold brew.
Choose the grind on the product page before adding to cart.

## Contact

Email hello@northwind-coffee.example or use this chat. Our team replies within 1 business day, Monday to Friday, 9am-5pm Eastern.
